Capsule CRMâs 14-day trial of Starter, Growth, Advanced and Ultimate starts as soon as you sign up. The trial includes every feature on the plan you choose, and you can add your team. No card is required.
What features are limited during the Capsule CRM trial?
None. During the 14-day Capsule CRM trial you can use every feature, connect any integration and add as many users as you need.
What happens at the end of the Capsule CRM trial?
When the 14-day Capsule CRM trial ends, you can stay on the plan you chose, switch plan, or cancel. To keep the data already in the account, add a payment card.
Is Capsule CRM free?
Yes. Capsule CRM has a free plan for up to 2 users and 250 contacts, with one sales pipeline.
You can move to a paid plan when you need a larger contact list or more features.
How much does Capsule CRM cost?
Capsule CRM has a free plan for up to 2 users and 250 contacts. Paid plans are priced per user, per month: Starter $18, Growth $36, Advanced $54 and Ultimate $72 when billed annually, or $22, $40, $60 and $75 billed monthly. Every paid plan starts with a free 14-day trial, with no card required.
Are there any setup or onboarding fees?
No. Capsule CRM has no setup fee and no onboarding fee. You can cancel at any time.
Can I upgrade or downgrade my Capsule plan?
Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade your Capsule CRM plan.
If you downgrade, you have to stay within the limits of the plan you move to.
Growth includes 60,000 contacts and Starter includes 30,000. Moving from Growth to Starter means reducing your contacts to 30,000.
Can I migrate my contacts into Capsule CRM?
Yes. You can import contacts and opportunities into Capsule CRM from a spreadsheet.

What does Capsule CRM onboarding look like?
Capsule CRM onboarding is a short in-app setup that starts when you open your account. There is no onboarding fee.
The setup walks you through getting started, and the support centre has articles on how the CRM works.
The support team can answer questions as they come up.

What does Capsule CRM integrate with?
Capsule supports 100+ integrations, and works with Zapier to fulfill additional functionality. Our most popular integrations include Transpond, Mailchimp, Xero, QuickBooks, Gmail and Office 365.
