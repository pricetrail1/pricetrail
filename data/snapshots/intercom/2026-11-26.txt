Pro is a set of advanced AI features for managing and improving your support operation. It includes Operator, an agent for customer operations, plus features that help you analyze performance, monitor quality, identify trends, and continuously improve Fin.
Credits are the unit used to measure and charge for Operator usage. Every Pro workspace receives monthly credits, and on contracted plans the number of credits included scales with conversation volume. If you use more credits than what’s included in Pro, you can buy additional credits at $0.01 per credit. Simple actions use fewer credits, while more complex work, like analyzing large sets of conversations, building automation, or testing changes, uses more. You can track usage, set alerts, and use hard limits to control spend.
