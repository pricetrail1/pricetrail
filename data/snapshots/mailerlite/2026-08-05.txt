Frequently asked questions
MailerLite offers 4 plans:
Free
Comfort
Power
Enterprise.
The
Free plan
is a generous way to get started, with a real taste of the full platform. You get up to 250 subscribers, 2 user seats, and 2,500 monthly emails, plus campaigns, automations, a website, a landing page, and signup forms, along with all 3 email editors (Drag & drop, Custom HTML, and Simple).
The
Comfort plan
(starting $12/month) is the full toolset at the right size for solopreneurs and small teams. You get everything in Free, plus unlimited templates, dynamic emails, campaign auto-resend, multivariate testing, the unsubscribe page builder, preference center, AI writing assistant, smart sending, and more, with sensible sending and usage limits.
The
Power plan
(starting $25/month) is for busy marketers with several revenue streams and sales funnels: sending regular deals and updates, deepening relationships through personalization, and earning more directly through MailerLite.
You get everything in Comfort, plus unlimited user seats, monthly emails, automations, forms, digital products, and bookings, multiple automation triggers, partner discounts, and 24/7 live chat support.
The
Enterprise plan
is a custom solution for businesses with over 200K subscribers that need dedicated support and services. Pricing depends on your active subscriber count and any add-ons. You get a dedicated success manager, dedicated IP (as an add-on), account audits and performance improvements, and deliverability support. Reach out through our
contact form
for details.
You can
switch plans
any time to the
Billing and Plan
tab in your account. Pick the plan that fits and get immediate access to your new features.
If you want to switch from an annual plan to a monthly plan, you can initiate the switch, but the change will not be effective until the annual plan has expired. Switching from monthly to annual is effective as soon as checkout is complete.
When downgrading your plan, you will need to wait until the billing cycle (monthly or annual) ends for the current plan, and the new (lesser) plan will start at the next billing cycle
Pricing is based on usage, not feature access. You pay for the number of subscribers you have and the volume you send. That's why our best tools are included on every paid plan, and your price scales as your list and sending grow.
We can only apply one discount at a time. However, we do offer a variety of discounts including annual and
nonprofit discounts
.
Billing is based on the number of
active subscribers
in your account. This includes anyone with an "active" status. Subscribers who have unsubscribed or bounced don't count.
To keep things fair, we use a cumulative count within each billing cycle: this means any address that was active at any point during the cycle counts toward your limit, even if it’s deleted later.
Your subscriber count resets once a month on your billing anniversary, set by the date of your first plan purchase. For example, if you delete subscribers on April 1st and your billing date is April 5th, those subscribers will continue to count toward your limit until April 4th.
For paid accounts
, when the number of active subscribers exceeds the current subscriber limit, the subscriber limit will automatically increase and your credit card will be charged for the next pricing tier. You will receive a notification in your account when the limit is close to being exceeded.
For free accounts
, when the number of active subscribers exceeds the 250 active subscriber limit, sendings will be locked (campaigns + automation). Clients will be prompted to sign up for a paid account, or they will need to remove active subscribers to regain sending ability.
Your monthly email limit depends on your plan and your subscriber tier.
Free
includes 2,500 emails per month.
Comfort
gives you 10× the ceiling of your subscriber tier each month. The multiplier is based on the top of your tier, not your exact subscriber count. So if you're on the up-to-1,000 tier, you get 10,000 emails a month whether you have 200 subscribers or 900. As your list grows into a higher tier, your sending limit scales up with it.
Power
includes unlimited monthly emails, subject to our fair use policy.
A "send" counts as one email to one subscriber, so a single campaign to 500 people uses 500 of your monthly emails. Your allowance resets each month on your billing date.
If you reach your Comfort limit before the month resets, you can upgrade to Power for unlimited sending, or wait until your next billing cycle when your allowance refreshes. We'll give you a heads-up in your account as you get close, so nothing catches you by surprise.
