“How do you count subscribers?” and other questions you might have…
Frequently asked questions
Billing is based on the number of active subscribers in your account which includes anyone with an "active" status. To keep things fair, we don’t count the same subscriber more than once even if they appear in multiple groups and subscribers who have unsubscribed or bounced don't count.
MailerLite offers 4 plans: Free, Comfort, Power, and Enterprise.
The
Free plan
is a generous way to get started, with a real taste of the full platform. You get up to 250 subscribers, 2 user seats, and 2,500 monthly emails, plus campaigns, automations, a website, a landing page, and signup forms, along with all 3 email editors (Drag & drop, Custom HTML, and Simple).
The
Comfort plan
(starting $12/month) is the full toolset at the right size for solopreneurs and small teams. You get everything in Free, plus unlimited templates, dynamic emails, campaign auto-resend, multivariate testing, the unsubscribe page builder, preference center, AI writing assistant, smart sending, and more, with sensible sending and usage limits.
The
Power plan
(starting $25/month) is for busy marketers with several revenue streams and sales funnels. You get everything in Comfort, plus unlimited user seats, automations, forms, digital products and bookings, multiple automation triggers, partner discounts, 24/7 live chat support, and unlimited sending up to 200,000 subscribers (fair use policy applies).
The
Enterprise plan
is a custom solution for businesses with 200,000+ subscribers that need dedicated support and services. Pricing depends on your active subscriber count and any add-ons. Reach out through our
contact form
for details.
Every new signup gets 14 days of free access to paid features, this is your trial, which is separate from the Free plan. When the 14 days are over, you move onto the Free plan automatically, at no cost and with no action needed.
If everything in your account fits within Free plan limits, it all keeps running as normal. If something's over a limit, your account will enter a restricted state until you upgrade or scale back, but nothing is deleted.
For example, if you built 5 automations during your trial, only 3 can stay active on Free; the other 2 are paused, not removed, so reactivating them is as simple as upgrading.
See “
What is included in MailerLite's 14-day premium trial
” for the full list of features you get access to during your trial.
Pricing is based on usage, not feature access. You pay for the number of subscribers you have and the volume you send. That's why our best tools are included on every paid plan, and your price adjusts as your list and sending needs grow.
Subscribers:
On a paid plan, going over your subscriber limit automatically moves you to the next pricing tier and your payment method is charged at a prorated rate. Don’t worry, we'll notify you in your account as you get close
On the Free plan, going over 250 active subscribers pauses sending (campaigns and automations) and new subscriber imports until you're back under the limit or you upgrade
Automations, forms, sites, landing pages, and digital products
Going over a feature limit means you can't activate or publish anything else until you upgrade or deactivate some of your automations, sites or digital products.
Email sends:
Surpassing your email sends on the Comfort plan (10x your subscriber limit) will automatically move you to the next pricing tier and your payment method will be charged at a prorated rate. Don’t worry, we'll notify you in your account as you get close.
On the Free plan, surpassing your 2,500 email sends in a month will pause sending for the rest of that month unless you upgrade.
In every case, we'll flag it in your account first, and nothing you've built is deleted; it's paused, not removed.
Your monthly email limit depends on your plan and your subscriber tier.
Free
includes 2,500 emails per month.
Comfort
gives you 10× the limit of your subscriber tier each month. The multiplier is based on the top of your tier, not your exact subscriber count, so if you're on the 1,000 subscriber tier you get 10,000 emails a month whether you have 600 subscribers or 900. For example, sending 2 newsletters a month to 1,000 subscribers is about 2,000 emails which is well inside that 10,000 allowance.
Power
includes unlimited monthly emails up to 200,000 subscribers, subject to our
fair use policy
.
A "send" counts as one email to one subscriber, so a single campaign to 500 people uses 500 of your monthly emails. Your allowance resets each month on your billing date. If you reach your Comfort limit before the month resets, you can upgrade to Power for unlimited sending, or wait until your next billing cycle.
