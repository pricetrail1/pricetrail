Will I pay more for some features?
Yes. We may provide some additional features that are not included in your plan, which you can subscribe to, but that's your choice to opt-in for them (eg. paid integrations for specific systems, additional contacts or more than 20 agents in your inbox).
Is VAT included in plan prices?
VAT is not included in our plan prices. As most Crisp customers are businesses, they do not owe VAT and thus we charge the advertised flat price. VAT is still due for consumers and may be applied on all plan prices (ie. non-business entities).
If you are an EU business, make sure you enter your EU VAT number to prove your business status and thus remove VAT from the price. If you are located outside the EU, we won't charge any VAT, regardless of your business or consumer status.
Do you offer discounts on multiple Crisp Workspaces?
Yes! We offer discounts if you are subscribing multiple paid Crisp Workspaces (Crisp Plus and Essentials only) under the same account. We offer 20% off for 3 additional Workspaces. Come chat with us to find the best deal!
What does a Crisp Workspace stand for?
A Crisp Workspace is the place where you can invite operators to collaborate and answer customer questions.
A Crisp workspace is tied to a unique identifier. Plans are billed per-Crisp workspace, not per domain and sub-domains.
As an example, you can have one Crisp workspace which refers to one subscription and connect with the following channels: Instagram, Messenger, or Whatsapp Business, Viber, Line, Telegram, Emails. One and only one of each, except emails, can be centralized in one workspace. You can connect more by subscribing to higher limits.
I’m running a nonprofit / I am a student. Do you offer discounts?
We offer a 50% discount for nonprofits and students. Chat with us to get your promo code.
Is it possible to pay yearly?
Yes, it is possible! You can enable yearly payment mode on your Plans page in the Crisp app after you subscribed to a plan (click on the Monthly button, which then becomes Yearly once saved).
Why are there no limits on the number of conversations?
At Crisp, we believe existing chat platforms are still locked in the past. Why would we bill based on consumption (as all others do), while we are used to unlimited plans everywhere else (mobile carriers, Internet access providers, etc.)? Unlimited is peace of mind at the end of the month; this allows you to allocate more energy to your core business rather than petty subscription policies.
