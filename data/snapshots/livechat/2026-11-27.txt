Pricing questions
Which plan is right for my team?
Essential:
For small teams earning their first revenue from chat. You get 1 AI Agent, 10 AI resolutions a month, and tracking for 50 live visitors. Conversation and file history: 60 days.
Growth:
For scaling teams that want chat to drive revenue. You get 10 AI Agents, 200 AI resolutions a month, and tracking for 400 live visitors. Conversation and file history: unlimited.
Enterprise:
For larger teams that need custom usage limits, single sign-on, or invoicing.
How does the free trial work, and what happens when it ends?
Try the full Growth plan free for 14 days. No credit card required. Add the chat widget to your website, invite colleagues, and run real conversations. To continue after the trial, choose a plan and billing period and add a payment method. You keep your workspace, chat history, and settings. If you don't continue, you won't be charged and have nothing to cancel.
What is the difference between monthly and annual billing?
Prices are per user per month. Annual billing covers 12 months, paid upfront. With monthly billing, you pay each month and can cancel anytime.
Essential costs $19 per user a month billed yearly, or $25 billed monthly.
Annual billing saves about 24%.
Growth costs $79 per user a month billed yearly, or $99 billed monthly.
Annual billing saves about 20%.
A five-person Growth team saves $1,200 a year with annual billing.
How much will a team of three or ten pay?
Three people on Essential: $57 a month billed yearly, or $75 billed monthly.
Three people on Growth: $237 a month billed yearly, or $297 billed monthly.
Ten people on Growth: $790 a month billed yearly, or $990 billed monthly.
On monthly billing, extra AI resolutions cost $49.50 per 50 each month. For ten people on Growth with 350 AI resolutions a month, monthly billing costs $1,138.50. The plan includes 200; three packages cover the rest. Calculate your cost with the
plan builder
.
Do I pay per user? How many users can I add?
Yes. You pay per user per month. Everyone who logs in to answer chats or tickets needs a seat. There's no minimum team size. You can start on Essential with one user. Essential and Growth have no fixed user cap for self-service teams. Choose Enterprise for larger teams or custom usage limits, single sign-on, or invoicing.
What counts as an AI resolution, and what if I need more?
One resolution = the AI directly solved a customer question. Multiple questions in the same chat still count as one.
Greetings, browsing, test chats, spam, and chats handed to your team without an answer don't count.
Essential includes 10 AI resolutions a month, and Growth includes 200.
Add extra packages of 50 AI resolutions from your billing settings whenever you need them.
A package of 50 costs $49.50 on both Essential and Growth.
You pay when you add a package and again at each renewal until you reduce it.
Your custom offer sets your Enterprise usage.
Text emails you when you reach 90% of your included resolutions. It never adds packages automatically at your limit. Unused resolutions don't carry over to the next billing cycle.
Are there any additional costs?
A package of 50 extra AI resolutions costs $49.50 on both plans.
You pay when you add a package and again at each renewal until you reduce it.
Add extra packages of 50 AI resolutions from your billing settings whenever you need them.
Essential includes 10,000 API calls a month, and Growth includes 20,000.
A package of 100,000 extra API calls costs $29.50 on both plans.
Buy 100,000 extra API calls anytime you need to scale up.
Some third-party
Marketplace
apps charge fees set by their developers. Sales tax may apply based on your business location.
Do I need to pay sales tax?
Prices exclude tax. If your business operates in a state where we must collect sales tax, we use your ZIP code to calculate it and add it to your invoice. Outside the United States, VAT or an equivalent local tax may apply under local rules.
How do plan changes and cancellation work?
You can upgrade, downgrade, or add and remove users anytime in the app's subscription settings. Downgrades take effect at your next renewal. These include a lower plan, fewer users, switching from annual to monthly billing, or fewer resolution packages. Your current plan and limits stay active until then. Monthly plans have no long-term contract. You can cancel anytime. Annual plans cover the 12 months you paid for. You can adjust or cancel them at renewal.
How do I read the comparison table?
Live visitor limits count the visitors you can watch at the same time. Teams, workflow rules, campaigns, and API calls are per account, so adding users doesn't lower anyone's allowance. Unused AI resolutions don't carry over to the next billing cycle.
What do all plans include?
Every plan includes CMS, CRM, and e-commerce integrations. You also get Website, In-app, Messenger, SMS (Twilio), AI Copilot, Workflows, 24/7/365 support, Web app, and Desktop app (macOS and Windows).
Compare conversation history, routing, reporting, AI allowances, and security features in the table above.
Can I use Text on several websites?
Yes. Essential, Growth, and Enterprise all let you run the chat widget on several websites from one account. Widget settings are saved per team.
Essential includes 1 AI Agent and 1 team. All sites share the same AI Agent and widget setup.
Growth includes 10 AI Agents and 10 teams, so each site can have its own.
Multilingual support is included in Growth and Enterprise.
Pick from 48 languages and assign dedicated agent groups per language.
Is the chat widget included, and can I customize it?
The website chat widget comes with Essential, Growth, and Enterprise.
All plans include Website widget translations, Live editor, Right-to-left support, Accessibility compliance, Dark mode, and Custom logo in website widget.
Install the widget in a few minutes with a code snippet or a plugin for platforms such as WordPress, Shopify, and Webflow. Follow the
installation guide
step by step.

Enterprise includes Custom AI Agents, Tailored workflow automation, Plugged into your data, Flexible scaling and usage limits, Support from Product Expert, Personalized onboarding, Product training for your team, Direct engineering support, SOC 2 Type 2, SLAs, legal and compliance review, White-label widget, and SSO and audit logs.
