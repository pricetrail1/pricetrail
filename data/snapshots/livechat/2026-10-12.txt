Pricing questions
Where is LiveChat pricing? Why am I seeing Text plans?
LiveChat pricing for new trials now lives here, as Text plans. Text is the next product from the team behind LiveChat. It keeps the live chat you came for and adds a shared inbox, ticketing, and an AI Agent in the same workspace, on one subscription. Nothing changes for existing LiveChat customers. Your LiveChat account, plan, and price stay exactly as they are, and you can keep
logging in here
. If you want to see how your current LiveChat plan compares, the current LiveChat plans stay on the
LiveChat pricing page

How does the free trial work, and what happens when it ends?
The free trial runs for 14 days on Essential and Growth and does not ask for a credit card. You get the full plan during the trial, so your team can put the chat widget on your website, invite colleagues, and run real conversations. At trial expiration you choose a plan and billing period and add a payment method, and your workspace, chat history, and settings carry over. Because no card is collected upfront, you are never charged automatically when the trial ends, and there is nothing to cancel if Text is not for you.

Do I pay per user? How many users can I add?
Yes. Text pricing is per user per month, and every user who logs in to answer chats or tickets needs a seat. There is no minimum team size, so a team of one can start on Essential. Essential and Growth have no fixed user cap for self-service teams. Larger teams, or teams that need custom usage limits, single sign-on, or invoicing, are a fit for the Enterprise plan.

Do I need to pay sales tax?
If you do business in a state where we are required to collect sales tax, we use your ZIP code to determine the correct tax rate and add it to your invoice. Prices on this page are shown before tax. Businesses outside the United States may be charged VAT or an equivalent local tax according to local rules.
How do plan changes and cancellation work?
You can upgrade, downgrade, or add and remove users at any time from the subscription settings in the app. Plan changes take effect on your next billing cycle. Monthly plans have no long-term contract and can be cancelled anytime. Annual plans run for the 12 months you paid for and can be adjusted or cancelled at renewal.
How do I read the comparison table?
A check means the feature is in the plan, a number is the plan limit, and a price marks an optional add-on. Visitor tracking limits are the number of live visitors you can watch at the same time. AI resolutions reset every month. Teams, workflow rules, campaigns, and API calls are per account, so adding people does not shrink anyone's allowance.
What do all plans include?
Features shared by all plans include Website, In-app, Messenger, SMS (Twilio), AI Copilot, Live Shopify catalog, Workflows, 24/7/365 support, Web app, and Desktop app (macOS and Windows).
See the comparison table for each plan's conversation history, routing,

The website chat widget is included in Essential, Growth, and Enterprise.
