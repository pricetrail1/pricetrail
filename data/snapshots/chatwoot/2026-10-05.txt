Every paid plan comes with free Captain AI credits each month: 300 for Startups, 500 for Business, and 800 for Enterprise. These credits cover features like reply suggestions, message summarization, and knowledge base answers. If you need more, additional credits are billed at $20 per 1,000. You can track your usage anytime in your billing dashboard.
Yes, we offer refunds for all Cloud plans. The refund amount is calculated based on your cancellation date and unused time. Note that Captain AI credits are non-refundable since they're usage-based. If you're not happy with Chatwoot for any reason, just reach out to us at sales@chatwoot.com.
You can upgrade anytime from your dashboard. Head to Settings → Billing, and you'll see all available plans along with what's included in each. You can also view your past invoices and update your payment method from the same page.
